Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-28

shorts-auto is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

Data accessed

  • Google account authorization (OAuth tokens) for the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, used solely to upload videos the owner has approved to the owner's own channels.
  • No other Google user data is requested, read, or stored.

Storage and sharing

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's local computer and are never transmitted to any third party.
  • No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time at Google Account permissions.

shorts-auto's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy apply.

개인정보처리방침 (요약)

이 도구는 소유자 본인만 사용하며, 본인이 승인한 영상을 본인 유튜브 채널에 올리는 데에만 youtube.upload 권한을 씁니다. 인증 토큰은 소유자의 컴퓨터에만 저장되고 제3자에게 전송·공유·판매되지 않습니다.
